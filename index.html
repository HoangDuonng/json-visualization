Frequently asked

JSON Visualization is an online tool designed to visualize and analyze various data formats, including JSON, YAML, CSV, XML and more. It transforms complex data into intuitive graphs, tree views, and interactive whiteboard diagrams, making it ideal for developers, data analysts, and anyone working with structured data.
The Draw view is a powerful whiteboard-style canvas that lets you visualize your JSON data as hand-drawn diagrams. It automatically converts your data structure into interactive nodes and arrows, giving you a more visual and creative way to explore relationships in your data. You can also freely draw, annotate, and rearrange elements on the canvas.
You can switch between views using the toggle buttons at the top of the editor. The Graph view provides a traditional structured layout, while the Draw view offers a free-form whiteboard experience where you can interact with your data visually.
Yes, JSON Visualization is a free-forever open source online tool.
Yes. When you paste or import your data into the editor, it's processed entirely in your browser to create the visualization. Your data never leaves your device or gets sent to any server.
A wide range of data formats are supported including JSON, YAML, XML, CSV, and TOML. You can also convert between these formats directly within the tool.
Yes, you can export your visualizations as PNG, JPEG, or SVG files from both the Graph view and the Draw view.
The Draw view includes a variety of drawing tools such as shapes (rectangles, diamonds, ellipses), arrows, lines, text, free-hand drawing, and more. You can customize colors, stroke styles, and arrange elements however you like.
Yes! JSON Visualization is open source. Visit our GitHub repository to contribute, report issues, or request features.
