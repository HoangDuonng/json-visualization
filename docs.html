Welcome to JSON Visualization

JSON Visualization is a powerful tool for visualizing, editing, and transforming JSON data. All processing happens in your browser - your data never leaves your device.

Get started by exploring our features below or jump straight to the Editor.

Features

Visualization

Transform JSON, YAML, CSV, XML into interactive graphs or tree views

Draw and annotate your JSON on a freeform canvas

Format Conversion

Convert between JSON, YAML, CSV, and XML formats seamlessly

Quick Start

1. Open the Editor

Visit the Editor and paste your JSON, YAML, CSV, or XML data.

2. Visualize Your Data

See your data transform into an interactive graph. Use Graph View for exploring relationships or Tree View for hierarchical structure.

3. Use Tools

Access powerful tools from the toolbar: convert formats, generate types, validate with JSON Schema, or query with jq/JSONPath.

4. Export & Share

Download your visualization as an image or export your transformed data.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Why is my JSON invalid?

Check for trailing commas, missing quotes, or unescaped characters. Use Format & Validate to pinpoint errors.

The graph is too dense or slow. What should I do?

Try Tree View, collapse large branches, or trim the dataset before visualizing.

CSV or XML doesn't look right after import.

Ensure CSV has a header row and consistent columns. For XML, verify proper nesting and closing tags.

How do I export a sharper image?

Zoom to fit before exporting. Use PNG for clarity or SVG for scalable output.

Is my data private?

Yes. All processing stays in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Privacy & Security
